Privacy Policy for AroidNode

Effective Date: September 24, 2026 | Application: AroidNode (com.aroidnode)

AroidNode ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is dedicated to protecting the personal data and botanical collection records of our users ("you"). This Privacy Policy transparently explains how we collect, store, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application AroidNode.

1. On-Device & Privacy-First Architecture

AroidNode is architected with a strict on-device data sovereignty philosophy. Your botanical specimen logs, growth journals, leaf variegation records, node lineages, and personal valuations are stored locally in your private on-device sandbox. We do not transmit or sell your botanical collection catalog to third-party brokers.

2. Information We Collect and Receive

  • Account Credentials (Google Sign-In): When you choose to authenticate via Google Sign-In, we receive your basic public profile information (Display Name, Email Address, and Avatar URL) to securely verify your curatorial identity and personalize your session.
  • Botanical Accession Photos & Media: When you photograph plant specimens, unfurling leaves, or attach certificates of authenticity, the images are stored locally on your device storage.
  • Subscription & Purchase Data: In-app subscriptions (such as Botanical Patron memberships) are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or bank details.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Telemetry: Anonymized crash logs and device metrics may be collected to ensure reliable application stability across various Android device form factors.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

AroidNode requests only the permissions strictly necessary to deliver its core curatorial features:

  • Camera: Required only when you actively choose to photograph botanical specimens or capture growth tracking milestones directly in the app.
  • Photos / Read Media Images: Required to select existing plant photographs from your gallery.
  • Storage / Documents: Used solely when you choose to export your botanical collection archive (JSON / CSV) or save generated accession reports.

4. Data Retention, Export & Deletion Rights

You maintain complete ownership and sovereignty over your data under global privacy frameworks (including GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play User Data Policy):

  • Instant Local Deletion: You can wipe all local specimen records and cached preferences at any time directly through the app settings.
  • Archive Export: You can export your complete botanical archive in open JSON format whenever you wish.
  • Account Deletion Request: If you wish to revoke Google Sign-In credentials and request complete erasure of any associated profile identifiers, you can submit a deletion request to support@aroidnode.com with the subject "Delete My AroidNode Account". Requests are fulfilled within 7 business days.

5. Children's Privacy

AroidNode is designed for rare plant enthusiasts, botanists, and collectors aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or data privacy requests regarding AroidNode, please contact our curatorial development team:

  • Email: support@aroidnode.com
  • Developer: AroidNode Team
